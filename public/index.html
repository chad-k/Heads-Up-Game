THE HOUSE RULES

Describe. Don’t say it.

  1. The guesser holds the phone facing friends.
  2. Give clues without saying the word or spelling it.
  3. Tilt down for correct; tilt up to skip.
  4. Return upright after every tilt.

Desktop: → or Space = correct, ← = skip, P = pause.
